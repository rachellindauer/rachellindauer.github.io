The definitive anime ranking

Every anime I’ve watched, ranked. Definitive means definitive. Disagreements can be sent to the email on the homepage, where they will be read and dismissed.

  1. Attack on Titan — 10/10

    Quite possibly the BEST show (anime or other) that I have ever watched. My first taste of anime. A hard act to follow.

  2. Fullmetal Alchemist — 8/10

    A great watch. Loved the character development and the story line. Felt a little bit predictable at times.

  3. Vinland Saga — 4/10

    I don’t get why every Reddit thread suggested this as a follow-up to Attack on Titan. The first season was decent and felt like a good story line, but the second season completely lost me. I had to watch the final episode three times because I kept falling asleep.
