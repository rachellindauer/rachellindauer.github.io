VJing

Why

My fiancé DJs in his free time, and I thought learning the visual art of live musical graphics would be a cool way for us to bond over his interests.

What

Learning to write GLSL shaders, the little GPU programs behind most real-time visual effects, using Shadertoy as the sandbox. The long-term rig is TouchDesigner synced to his Ableton, so the visuals lock to his BPM live.

The style I’m chasing is my own: monochromatic, organic, growing textural fields that breathe with the music. I have a fine-arts background, and my hand-drawn work explored dense fields of thousands of tiny packed cells; this is that drawing, learning to move. Deliberately apart from the neon-kaleidoscope mainstream of festival visuals. Texture over symmetry, restraint over spectacle.
